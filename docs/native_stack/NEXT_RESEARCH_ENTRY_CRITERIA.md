# Next research entry criteria

P2-R1 may start only after `P2_NATIVE_STACK_BENCHMARK_READY` is frozen and tagged. P2-R1 is limited to developing and freezing strong Native-PID, Native-Full-LQR, Native-Task-LQR, and SATC-Native baselines inside this protocol.

P2-R1 must not modify V1-V10 controllers or evidence, and it must not execute Native Holdout. It must preregister Equal-Rate versus Native-Rate use, compute deadlines, search budgets, gates, and Development-only selection.

Paper selection and implementation belong only to a later authorized P2-R2 after native traditional and SATC baselines are frozen. P2-R0 selects no paper and grants no authorization to begin P2-R1 automatically.
